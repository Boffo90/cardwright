Cardwright turns card images into true 1200 DPI print-ready proxies, using AI
upscaling on your own GPU, and lays them out into sheets you can cut by hand or
with a cutting machine. It handles **Magic, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh and Riftbound**,
and any card size you give it in millimetres.

This wiki has the longer guides. The app's own **Help** button carries a
shorter FAQ, and the [README](https://github.com/Boffo90/cardwright#readme)
lists every feature.
